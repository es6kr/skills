# Doc Adapter Topic (`doc.md`)

## Documentation Execution & Output Adapters

Executes document generation, reporting, and knowledge synthesis tasks.

### Supported Outputs
1. **Markdown Knowledge Synthesis**: Curating validated knowledge into `pages/<domain>/*.md` and updating `index.md`.
2. **Document Conversion**: Integration with `docxport` for Word export and `quote` for business quotations.
3. **Executive Reporting**: Structuring professional Markdown tables, Mermaid charts, and decision memos without unauthorized decorative emojis.
