# Proposal Topic (`proposal.md`)

## 1. Scope

While engineering plans focus on technical changes, organizational and business initiatives require formal proposals, requisitions, and decision records.

---

## 2. Proposal Document Structure

Author proposal documents as `proposal-<topic>.md` using standard frontmatter:

```yaml
---
title: "Proposal: <Proposal Title>"
created: YYYY-MM-DD
status: draft
topic: <topic-slug>
language: en
relates_to:
  - "https://plane.es6.kr/es6kr/browse/<ID>"
---
```

### Core Sections:
1. **Executive Summary**: Core objective, requested budget/resources, and timeline.
2. **Business Context & Justification**: Why this initiative is needed now and what risk is incurred by deferring it.
3. **Proposed Solution & Options**: Detailed description of recommended option vs alternatives.
4. **Budget & Resource Requirements**: Cost breakdown (subscriptions, infrastructure, licensing).
5. **Implementation Milestones**: Phased delivery schedule with clear milestones.
6. **Approval & Sign-off**: Designated stakeholders, review criteria, and sign-off table.
